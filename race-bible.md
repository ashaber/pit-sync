## The Race

Lap format endurance race. Course is roughly 9–10 miles in length. Start promptly at 9am. All laps completed by 5pm are counted.

2026 course includes the lower portion of Vandelay trail — adds tight and sometimes technical turns, additional distance and climbing compared to 2025.

### Profile Summary
- **Miles 0–1:** Hard climb out of start, gain ~260ft
- **Miles 1–3:** Sustained high plateau ~5,590ft — find rhythm here
- **Miles 3.9–4.8:** Course crux — peak at 5,663ft, sharp descent
- **Miles 5–6:** Recovery descent then climb back to 5,600ft
- **Miles 6.7–7.7:** Significant descent ~485ft
- **Miles 7.7–9.6:** Vandelay — tight technical turns, gradual return to start

### Vandelay (new 2026)
Added to end of lap. Tight and sometimes technical. Rides easy fresh — demanding when fatigued at race pace. Scout Friday evening preview lap. Descent into finish is recovery, not a time trial.
