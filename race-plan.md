## Pacing — Power & HR

### The One Rule
> **Never start a climb already in Z3.5. Come into climbs recovered.**

### Garmin 540 Screen Setup
- **Primary:** 3s power (large) | Lap avg power | HR zone | Lap timer
- **Secondary:** Course map
- Auto-lap: GPS trigger at start/finish coordinate

### By Terrain
| Terrain | Power | HR Zone | Notes |
|---------|-------|---------|-------|
| Sustained climb | 205–220W | Z3, cap Z3.9 | Z4 before lap 5 = back off |
| Punchy steep | ≤270W | Z4 briefly | Must return Z3 within 90 sec |
| Flat / rolling | 150–170W | Z2.5–Z3 | Z3.5 here = too hard |
| Descent / Vandelay | Soft pedal | Z2 or below | Recovery — not a time trial |

### Warning Signs
- Z4 on sustained climb before lap 5 → back off immediately
- HR not returning below Z3 within 90 sec after punch → ease next section
- Z3.5 on flat terrain → back off
- Still Z3 on descent → something is wrong upstream

## Layers Protocol
**Lesson from 2025: you froze when snow hit lap 5. Don't repeat.**

- Renee reads weather proactively — offers layer at next pit
- Put layers on one lap early. Remove one lap early.
- Don't wait until cold.

### Layer Change Rules
- **Andrew removes a layer at pit:** Renee confirms Andrew is still carrying at least one layer before he departs. If he's stripping down to race kit, Renee offers to keep one item staged on the bike or in a pocket.
- **Andrew requests additional layer:** Renee checks the kit bag has a backup before handing anything over. Do not empty the bag — Andrew may need more later.
- **Goal:** Andrew always leaves the pit with appropriate layers for conditions. Kit bag always has at least one item remaining.

| Item | Deploy When |
|------|------------|
| Wind vest | Temperature drops, pre-storm sky |
| Arm warmers | Sustained cloud cover, cold descents |
| Rain jacket | Rain starting or imminent |
| Light gloves | Hands losing feel on descents |
